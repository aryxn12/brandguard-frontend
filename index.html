Live demo runbook

A short, repeatable story for judges.
3–4 MIN
  1. Problem: teams can publish risky copy, expose customer data or follow hostile instructions.
  2. Solution: submit content to BrandGuard for policy-aware AI review before publication.
  3. Show three cases: clean copy should pass; PII / competitor / guaranteed-return claims should be flagged or blocked; prompt injection should be isolated and rejected.
  4. Close the loop: inspect the explanation and evidence, sign off the reviewed version, then refresh the audit trail to show the backend record and hash.
For judging: describe only results actually returned by the live backend. Offline or disconnected states are clearly marked and are not counted as approvals.
